## Why can't I edit or delete something?

Your role may not allow it. Check [roles at a glance](../roles/overview.md). Ask your administrator if you need more access.

## Can other companies see my data?

No. Each organization's data is separate. You only see records that belong to your organization.

## Who approves a change order?

Administrators approve or reject change orders. Managers raise them.

## How do I change my password?

Open your profile from your initials at the top right and change it there. See [signing in](../getting-started/signing-in.md).

## Which currency and taxes does the app use?

Those set by your administrator's regional preset. See [regional settings](../administration/regional-settings.md).

## Who do I contact for help?

Start with your organization's administrator.
