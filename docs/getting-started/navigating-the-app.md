# Finding Your Way Around

Mansion is organized so you can reach any site record, contract, or financial report in two clicks or taps.

The interface is structured into **4 Core Sections**, each containing dedicated tools for daily construction tasks.

---

## The 4 Main Menu Sections

![Mansion Desktop Navigation](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/samuelek25k/mansion-docs@main/screenshots/01_executive_dashboard.png)

### 1. Dashboard (The High-Altitude Overview)
- **What it is**: The cockpit for portfolio health.
- **Key pages**: Project cost and profitability overview, health radar scores, and financing exposure.
- **Who uses it most**: Company executives, project managers, and investors checking overall progress.

### 2. Site Operations (The Job-Site Hub)
- **What it is**: Everything happening physically on the ground across your active sites.
- **Key pages**:
  - **Projects Directory**: List of all active and planned building jobs.
  - **Subcontracts & Subleasing**: Specialist trade packages (electrical, plumbing) and equipment rentals (cranes, excavators).
  - **Change Orders**: Formal requests to add extra work or change specifications.
  - **Daily Field Diary**: Headcounts, weather logs, delays, and concrete slump test checks.
  - **Site Schedule**: Upcoming milestones and target completion dates.

### 3. Financials (Money & Cash Flow)
- **What it is**: Strict commercial cost governance and billing.
- **Key pages**:
  - **Cost Control & EVM**: Tracking actual spend against estimated budget ceilings.
  - **Milestone Invoices & Retainage**: Progress payment billing to clients and defect liability escrow ledgers.
  - **Cash Flow & General Ledger**: Direct bank RTGS settlements and site petty cash expenses.
  - **Material Stock Movements**: Tracking deliveries, inter-site transfers, and usage from the central yard to job sites.

### 4. Workforce & Masters (People & Catalogs)
- **What it is**: Your contacts, workers, and price catalogs.
- **Key pages**:
  - **Vendors & Trade Partners**: Approved suppliers and subcontractors with tax/license records.
  - **Labour & Subcontractors**: Daily wage muster rolls, worker trade categories, and attendance logs.
  - **Catalogs & Master Data**: Unit rates, standard material items (cement, rebar, aggregates), and cost codes.

## Working on Desktop vs Mobile

### On Desktop & Tablets
On wider screens, the top navigation bar displays the 4 main sections. Selecting any section immediately reveals its sub-tabs directly underneath.

### On Mobile Phones (Field-First Ergonomics)
When opening Mansion on a smartphone at the job site:
- A persistent **Bottom Navigation Dock** provides one-thumb switching between the 4 sections.
- All touch buttons are sized to at least **44x44 pixels**, making them easy to tap even when wearing work gloves on site.

![Mobile Navigation Dock](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/samuelek25k/mansion-docs@main/screenshots/06_mobile_experience.png)

- See the full [Mobile Experience Guide](../user-guide/mobile-experience.md) for field tips.

## Why Can't I See a Button or Action?

If you notice an action button is missing (for example, "+ Create Project", "Approve Change Order", or "Delete Record"):
- Mansion automatically hides buttons that your assigned role is not permitted to perform.
- For example, Site Managers can *request* change orders, but only Administrators can *approve* them.
- Check the [Roles at a glance](../roles/overview.md) page to confirm what your role permits.
