The main menu has four sections. Each section has its own row of pages directly below the menu.

| Section | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | Project cost and profitability overview |
| **Site Operations** | Projects Directory, Subcontracts & Subleasing, Change Orders, Daily Field Diary, Site Schedule |
| **Financials** | Cost Control & EVM, Milestone Invoices & Retainage, Cash Flow & General Ledger, Material Stock Movements |
| **Workforce & Masters** | Vendors & Trade Partners, Labour & Subcontractors, Catalogs & Master Data |

Administrators also see an organization settings page from their profile menu.

## On a phone

A navigation bar at the bottom of the screen gives one-tap access to each section, with large touch targets for use on site.

## Why can't I see a button?

Buttons for actions you are not allowed to perform are hidden or rejected. This is by design: see [roles at a glance](../roles/overview.md) to check what your role can do.
