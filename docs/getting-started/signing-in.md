# Signing In & Profile Setup

Accessing Mansion is fast, secure, and works seamlessly whether you are on a laptop in the office or a smartphone on a job site.

---

## How to Sign In (Step-by-Step)

1. **Open your web browser**: Navigate to the Mansion URL provided by your administrator (e.g. `https://yourcompany.mansionpms.com`).
2. **Click Sign In**: In the top navigation bar or mobile menu, click the **Sign In** button.
3. **Enter your credentials**:
   - **Email Address**: Use your official work email.
   - **Password**: Enter your temporary or permanent password.
4. **Access your dashboard**: Click **Sign In**. Once authenticated, you will immediately land on the **Executive Dashboard** tailored with your assigned permissions.

:::note Organization Privacy
Signing in securely connects you to your company's workspace. You will only see projects, contracts, invoices, and teammates belonging to your organization.
:::

---

## Verifying Your Role & Active Jurisdiction

Once signed in, look at the top navigation bar:
- **Initials Badge (Top Right)**: Shows your initials (e.g., `SJ` for Samuel Mathew) and your active user profile.
- **Jurisdiction Pill (Top Header)**: Displays your company's active country setting (e.g., `IN` for India, `AE` for UAE, `US` for USA). This determines the currency symbol (`₹`, `$`, `£`, `AED`) and tax rates applied throughout the app.

## Managing Your Account

### Changing Your Password
It is good security practice to update your password when you first receive your account:
1. Click your **Initials Badge** at the top right of the screen to open the **User Profile & Settings** modal.
2. Select **Change Security Password →**.
3. Type your **Current Password**, followed by your **New Password** (minimum 8 characters).
4. Click **Confirm Password Change**.

### Updating Your Name and Profile
To update how your name appears on site diaries, change order sign-offs, and payment vouchers:
1. Click your **Initials Badge**.
2. Edit your **Full Name** field.
3. Click **Save Changes**. Your name updates across all live logs and inspection sheets instantly.

## Troubleshooting Sign-In Issues

| Symptom | Cause | Solution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **"Invalid Credentials" error** | Misspelled email or incorrect password. | Check for accidental trailing spaces, confirm Caps Lock is off, and verify your email spelling. |
| **"Session Expired" alert** | Tokens expire after periods of inactivity for security. | Refresh the page and sign in again. Any saved work remains completely safe in the database. |
| **"Account Inactive" message** | Your profile may not have been activated by your administrator. | Contact your company administrator or site manager to confirm your role is active in the Team directory. |
| **Cannot see specific buttons** | Your assigned role does not permit that action. | See [Roles at a glance](../roles/overview.md) to check what permissions your role includes. |
