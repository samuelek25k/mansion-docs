title: Signing in
---

1. Open the Mansion address your administrator gave you.
2. Choose **Sign In**, then enter your email and password.
3. After signing in you land on the **Dashboard**.

## Change your password

Open your profile from your initials at the top right of the screen and choose the option to change your password. You need your current password to set a new one.

## Update your profile

Your profile also lets you update your name and contact details.

:::tip Cannot sign in?
Check that your email is typed correctly and that Caps Lock is off. If it still fails, ask your administrator to confirm your account is active.
:::
