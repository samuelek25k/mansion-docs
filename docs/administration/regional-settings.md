title: Regional settings
---

Tax schedules, currency and unit standards adapt to each region. Five presets are available.

| Region | Currency | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| India (RERA and GST) | INR | 18% GST with CGST/SGST split, 2% TDS on contractor payments, 5% retention, cement in 50kg bags and sand in cubic feet |
| UAE (FIDIC and FTA) | AED | 5% VAT, 10% standard retainage, metric units, TRN tax identification |
| Saudi Arabia (ZATCA) | SAR | 15% VAT, 10% retainage escrow, GOSI compliance tags, Arabic locale and number formatting |
| USA (AIA) | USD | AIA G702/G703 billing, 10% retainage with lien waivers, cement in 94lb bags, EIN and state sales tax |
| UK (HMRC CIS and JCT) | GBP | 20% CIS withholding, 20% VAT, 5% JCT retention release, tonnes and square metres |
