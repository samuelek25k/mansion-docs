Everyone in your organization has one of five roles: Administrator, Manager, Accountant, Auditor or Viewer.

## See who is on your team

Administrators can open the team list to see each person and their role.

## Choosing the right role

| Person | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Runs a site or project | Manager |
| Handles billing, vouchers and vendors | Accountant |
| Reviews records for compliance | Auditor |
| Client or stakeholder who follows progress | Viewer |
| Runs the organization's setup in Mansion | Administrator |

Give people the least access that lets them do their job. See [roles at a glance](../roles/overview.md) for exactly what each role can do.

## Change orders and approvals

Change orders are approved by administrators. Managers raise them and track the outcome.
