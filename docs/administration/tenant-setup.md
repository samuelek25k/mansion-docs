# Setting Up Your Organization

As the company **Administrator**, you have the master keys to Mansion. You configure your company profile once, and the system automatically adapts currency symbols, statutory tax calculations, retention policies, and measurement units across every project.

:::info Who Can Use This
**Administrators Only**. Settings configured here apply across your entire company.
:::

## First-Time Setup Checklist (In 6 Steps)

Follow this quick setup list to get your company ready in less than 10 minutes:

### 1. Set Your Legal Company Identity
- Click your **Initials Badge** (top right) → **Super Admin: Tenant Console → Regional Compliance & Currency Settings**.
- Enter your **Tenant Legal Display Name** (e.g., *SR Construction Group Ltd*). This name appears on all client invoices and official payment certificates.

### 2. Enter Your Official Tax Registration
- Enter your country's legal tax identification number:
  - **India**: 15-digit GSTIN (e.g., `32AAACA1234A1Z5`).
  - **UAE / Saudi Arabia**: 15-digit TRN or VAT Certificate Number.
  - **USA**: Federal Employer Identification Number (EIN).
  - **UK**: HMRC VAT Registration Number / UTR.

### 3. Choose Your Regional Preset
- Pick your jurisdiction preset from the dropdown (India, UAE, Saudi Arabia, USA, or United Kingdom).
- Mansion instantly configures:
  - Standard tax rates (e.g. 18% GST or 5% VAT)
  - Default defect retention withholding (5% or 10%)
  - Local currency symbols (`₹`, `AED`, `SAR`, `$`, `£`)
  - Material units of measurement (metric m³ vs imperial cubic yards)
- For details, see the [Regional Settings Guide](./regional-settings.md).

### 4. Load Master Data & Catalogs
- Add your frequent suppliers, trade subcontractors, and standard materials (cement, rebar, aggregates) via **Workforce & Masters → Catalogs & Master Data**. See [Master Data](./master-data.md).

### 5. Create Your First Project
- Open **Site Operations → Projects Directory** and click **+ Create New Project**.
- Input the agreed contract budget ceiling, target completion date, and client name. See [Projects Directory](../user-guide/projects.md).

### 6. Invite Your Site Team
- Add your site engineers, superintendents, and accountants, assigning them their specific roles (`manager`, `accountant`, `auditor`, or `viewer`). See [Team & Roles](./team-and-roles.md).

## Ongoing Administrator Responsibilities

Once set up, the administrator's daily and weekly tasks remain simple and lean:
- **Approve or Reject Change Orders**: Review scope variations submitted by site managers to keep budgets balanced.
- **Review Executive Radar**: Monitor the dashboard weekly to ensure actual expenses stay well behind billed client revenue.
- **Maintain Data Hygiene**: Decommission completed projects and archive old supplier accounts.
