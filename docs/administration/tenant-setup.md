As the administrator you set up your organization once, then keep it tidy.

## First-time checklist

1. **Set your company details.** Open organization settings from your profile menu. Enter the company display name and your tax registration number (for example a GSTIN or TRN).
2. **Choose your region.** Pick the regional preset that matches where you operate. It sets tax, currency, retention and units. See [regional settings](./regional-settings.md).
3. **Review lists and labels.** Adjust status names and colours to match how your team talks. See [lists and labels](./lists-and-labels.md).
4. **Load master data.** Add customers, vendors, stock items and budget items. See [master data](./master-data.md).
5. **Create your first project.** Open **Site Operations → Projects Directory** and add a project with its customer, dates and budget.
6. **Confirm your team.** Check the team list so everyone has the right role. See [your team](./team-and-roles.md).

## Ongoing responsibilities

- Approve or reject [change orders](../user-guide/change-orders.md) raised by managers.
- Remove outdated projects, customers, vendors and labour records. Only administrators can delete them.
- Certify payment claims alongside accountants.
- Keep an eye on the dashboard for projects approaching their budget ceiling.
