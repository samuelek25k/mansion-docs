---

Master data is the shared catalog every project draws on. Find it under **Workforce & Masters → Catalogs & Master Data**.

| Catalog | Used for |
| --- | --- |
| Customers | Linking projects and invoices to clients |
| Vendors | Subcontracts, purchases and vendor insights |
| Stock items | Material issues and stock movements |
| Budget items | Building project budgets and cost codes |

Administrators and accountants can add records. Only administrators can delete customers and vendors, so duplicates are cleaned up in one place.

:::tip
Agree naming conventions with your accountants before loading data, such as a vendor's full legal name, so searches stay predictable.
:::
