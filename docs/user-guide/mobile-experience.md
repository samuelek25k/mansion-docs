# Mobile-First Field Ergonomics

Construction doesn't happen behind an office desk—it happens outdoors on active job sites surrounded by dust, noise, scaffolding, and moving equipment. 

Mansion is built from the ground up to be **field-first**. You don't need a bulky laptop; site supervisors, foremen, and project engineers can manage their entire site directly from a smartphone or ruggedized field tablet.

:::info Who Can Use This
**Everyone**. The mobile interface automatically adapts whenever you open Mansion on a mobile device or tablet browser.
:::

## Why Mobile Ergonomics Matter on a Construction Site

Standard corporate software is frustrating to use outdoors. Mansion is designed specifically around real-world site conditions:

| Real-World Challenge | Mansion Solution |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wearing Work Gloves** | Every button, navigation item, and modal toggle has a minimum **44x44 pixel touch target** to prevent mis-taps when using gloves. |
| **Bright Outdoor Sunlight** | High-contrast visual styling and clear color coding ensure screens remain legible under direct midday sunlight. |
| **Holding a Phone in One Hand** | A persistent **Bottom Navigation Dock** places the 4 primary modules right under your thumb for effortless one-handed navigation. |
| **Fast-Paced Site Routine** | Optimized data entry flows let supervisors log daily worker muster rolls or concrete slump test results in under **60 seconds**. |
| **Disruptive Popups** | Replaces jarring modal popups with non-blocking **Toast Alerts** that quietly confirm actions in the background. |

## Core Mobile Navigation Elements

When viewing Mansion on a mobile screen:

1. **Top Bar**:
   - Shows the company monogram, brand name, and your active regional jurisdiction badge (`IN`, `AE`, `US`, `UK`, `SA`).
   - Quick-access user initials badge to open your profile or sign out.
2. **Scrollable KPI Cards**:
   - High-priority metrics (Health Radar score, Net Portfolio Margin, and Cash Financing Gap) format into swipeable, vertical summary cards.
3. **One-Tap Bottom Dock**:
   - **Dashboard**: Instant health score and cash status.
   - **Operations**: Daily field diaries, change orders, and project directories.
   - **Financials**: Milestone invoicing, cash flow, and material stock transfers.
   - **Workforce**: Labour muster rolls and vendor contacts.

## Practical Field Tips for Site Foremen

:::tip Best Practices for the Field
- **Add to Home Screen**: In Chrome or Safari on your phone, tap **Share / Options → Add to Home Screen** for instant one-tap access like a native app.
- **Log as you walk**: Don't wait until the end of the day to write your diary. Record worker headcounts and weather delays directly on site while inspecting each floor.
- **Save battery**: The dark theme significantly reduces OLED screen battery drain during long 10-hour site shifts.
:::
