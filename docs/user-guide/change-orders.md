title: Change orders
---

:::info Who can use this
Everyone can view. Administrators and managers raise requests. Administrators approve or reject.
:::

The change order register prevents scope creep with client variation sign-offs, schedule delay assessments and contract additions. Managers request variations; administrators approve or reject them.
