Reconcile procurement commitments, plant hire rates and progress payment applications against baseline cost ceilings.

## Trade subcontracts

Manage MEP, structural steel, glazing and waterproofing packages with original contract values, approved variations and automated retention withholding. Revised contract value, defect retention of 5% to 10%, and scope amendment linking are tracked per package.

## Equipment and machinery sublease

Sublease tower cranes, hydraulic excavators, mobile batching plants and scaffolding sets with flexible rental terms and operator inclusion. Rates can be monthly, daily or hourly, with mobilization and demobilization fees and third-party safety certifications.

## Payment claims and earned value

Issue Interim Payment Certificates (IPC) with work-completed verification, materials on site and backcharge contra-deductions. Earned value reporting covers the cost breakdown structure, estimate at completion and protected profit contingency.
