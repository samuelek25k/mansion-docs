For commercial and finance officers who manage money, contracts and master data.

## Your daily routine

1. **Record money in and out.** In **Cash Flow & General Ledger**, add receipts and payments as vouchers. You can also delete a mistaken entry.
2. **Bill progress.** Review **Milestone Invoices & Retainage** and track balances due and retention held.
3. **Collect payment.** Start an online payment for an invoice that has a balance due.
4. **Certify claims.** Review subcontract payment claims and certify them in **Subcontracts & Subleasing**.

## Master data

Keep **Catalogs & Master Data** accurate: customers, vendors, stock items and budget items. You can add records; an administrator removes them.

## Stock

You can move and issue stock. Your administrator may restrict stock issue for finance officers.

## Cost control

Add cost codes and review earned value (CPI, SPI and estimate at completion) in **Cost Control & EVM**.

## What you cannot do

Approve change orders, create or delete projects, or change organization settings. Ask an administrator.
