For clients and stakeholders who follow progress.

## What you can see

- **Dashboard**: overall progress and project health.
- **Milestone Invoices & Retainage**: approved milestones and their status.
- Project, schedule and diary pages in read-only form.

## What to expect

Viewing is read-only. You can browse everything available to you but you cannot make changes. If you need something changed, contact the project manager or your organization's administrator.
