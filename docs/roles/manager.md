For project managers and site foremen who run day-to-day work on site.

## Your daily routine

1. **Record the day.** Open **Site Operations → Daily Field Diary** and log labour headcount, weather, temperature, work performed, and any site delays.
2. **Take attendance.** Mark who worked, using the bulk attendance entry for your site's labour.
3. **Plan ahead.** Add or update milestones and activities in **Site Schedule**.
4. **Issue and move materials.** Use **Material Stock Movements** to transfer stock between sites or issue it to a site.

## Projects and scope

- Create and edit projects in the **Projects Directory**. Only an administrator can delete one.
- Raise a **change order** when scope, cost or time changes. An administrator approves or rejects it. See [change orders](../user-guide/change-orders.md).

## Contracts and cost control

- Create subcontracts and submit payment claims under **Subcontracts & Subleasing**. A claim is certified by an administrator or accountant.
- Add cost codes to a project in **Cost Control & EVM**.

## Labour

Add and edit people in **Labour & Subcontractors**. Ask an administrator to remove a record.

## What you can only view

The cash ledger, invoices and retainage are visible to you but are managed by accountants and administrators.
