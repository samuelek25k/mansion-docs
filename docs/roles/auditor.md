For compliance and financial auditors who verify records without changing them.

## What you can open

- **Dashboard** for portfolio cost and profitability.
- **Cash Flow & General Ledger** for all recorded transactions.
- **Milestone Invoices & Retainage** for billing and retention balances.
- Contracts, change orders, stock movements and project records in read-only form.

## How to verify

1. Start from the dashboard and note the figure you are verifying.
2. Open the ledger and filter to the project, then trace transactions to their vouchers.
3. Check invoices and retainage against the contract values.
4. Compare cost variance on the project's cost control page.

You cannot create, edit or delete anything, so your review never alters the records.
